# SaMaWi Dating MVP

## Product goal

Build a local-first dating platform where users can find real nearby adults, state their intentions clearly and communicate without pay-per-message mechanics.

## MVP areas

1. Accounts and 18+ registration
2. Public dating profiles
3. Profile photos
4. Dating intentions
5. Interests and profile questions
6. Privacy-preserving local/radius search
7. Likes and matches
8. Conversations and messages
9. Activity status
10. Blocks and reports
11. Verification state
12. Moderation and anti-scam risk signals

## Anti-scam baseline

The system should detect or restrict high-risk behavior such as:

- a new account immediately sending phone numbers, email addresses, URLs or messenger handles;
- repeated or near-identical messages sent to many users;
- rapid bulk messaging;
- attempts to obfuscate external contact details;
- repeated reports or blocks.

External contact exchange must not be permanently prohibited. Trusted users in an established conversation should be able to exchange contact details.

## Payments / SMW Donate

Not part of the MVP. Donation support is considered only after the core dating platform is operational and reliable.
